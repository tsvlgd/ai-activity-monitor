A GNOME Shell extension for coding agents

Your coding agents, at a glance.

GlanceAgent puts a small live icon for every Claude Code and OpenCode session in the top bar and on your desktop. It moves while the agent works, hops when it needs you, and goes still when there is nothing to do.
